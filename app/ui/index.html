    Phone deck

    Warcry serves a page your phone opens over your own network. It shows a button for every command you tick as a phone button, and a cast/reel pair for every World of Warcraft window it can hear — so a multiboxed client gets its own fish button.

        Character

        Warcry reads the macro cache World of Warcraft writes when you log out, so a character you have not played since the client started may look out of date. Nothing is ever written back into your install: copy a macro here and paste it into the game's own macro window.
