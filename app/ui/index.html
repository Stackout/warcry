Let’s get you set up

Create a profile to start. The fishing example casts and reels with your voice, so it’s a good place to begin.

    Say a command. What Warcry hears shows up here.

    Alternatives go in brackets, separated by ;, and an empty one means optional: heal [my;the;] pet accepts "heal my pet", "heal the pet" and "heal pet". Separate whole phrasings with ; outside brackets.

    Click a key field and press the combination you want — no typing. Esc cancels.

      Phone deck

      Warcry serves a page your phone opens over your own network. It shows a button for every command you tick as a phone button, and a cast/reel button for each World of Warcraft window it can hear.
